Form endpoint test

This posts the exact same payload as the home page form, but into a visible frame so you can read Google's reply. Submit it, then look at the panel below.

What you should see: Google's "Your response has been recorded" page means it worked. A sign-in page, "You need permission", or the blank form reloaded means it was rejected.

If it was rejected, check these in the Google Form:

  1. Settings → Responses → Collect email addresses must be Responder input. If it's Verified, off-site submissions are blocked.
  2. Settings → Responses → Limit to 1 response must be OFF (it forces sign-in).
  3. Settings → Restrict to users in your organization must be OFF.
  4. Every required question must be one of the five fields above. A required question we don't send will silently reject the whole submission.
