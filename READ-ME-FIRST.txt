Turning Pages on
  Repo > Settings > Pages > Build and deployment
  Source: Deploy from a branch
  Branch: main, folder: / (root) > Save
  The site appears at https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo>/ in a minute or two.

Note
  Client pages are unlisted, not private. Anyone with the link can open them,
  and they carry noindex so search engines skip them.
